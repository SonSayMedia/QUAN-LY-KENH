Privacy Policy

Quan Ly Kenh · operated by Sonsay Media · Last updated: 21 September 2026

Quan Ly Kenh (“the app”) is a private dashboard that lets a YouTube channel owner view statistics, analytics and comments of the channels they own. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and how you can control it. Back to the home page.

1. Information the app accesses

When you connect a channel, Google asks you to grant the app the following read-only permissions:

  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): your channel details (name, subscriber and view counts) and the list and public statistics of your videos.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): analytics reports for your channel and videos (views, watch time, average view duration, traffic sources, countries, devices, audience demographics, audience retention).
  • YouTube Analytics monetary read-only (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): estimated revenue reports for channels that are monetised.

The app also reads public information from YouTube, such as public comments on your videos, using the YouTube Data API.

The app does not request permission to upload, edit or delete videos, change channel settings, or post, edit or delete comments, and it does not post anything on your behalf.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to show you your own channel data inside the app: statistics, charts, rankings, alerts about a channel that needs attention, and the list of comments that have not been answered yet. Optionally, the text of a public comment may be sent to an AI translation service to produce a translation and a suggested reply for you to review; nothing is posted automatically.

The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not transfer it to third parties except as described in this policy or where required by law.

3. Where the information is stored

The app runs locally on the channel owner's own computer. Data received from Google (including the access token used to keep the connection) is stored in files on that computer and is not uploaded to any server operated by Sonsay Media. The app has no accounts, no database in the cloud and no analytics or tracking of its own.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not shared with other people or companies. The only exception is the optional AI translation described above, which processes the text of a public comment to return a translation and a draft reply.

5. Your choices, retention and deletion

  • You can withdraw access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Inside the app, choosing “Disconnect” for a channel revokes the access with Google and deletes that channel's stored data (statistics, analytics snapshots and comment lists) from the computer.
  • Access that is revoked or expires stops working immediately, and stored data for that channel is deleted when it is disconnected.

6. Security

The app listens only on the owner's own computer and is not reachable from other devices. Access tokens are stored in local files on that computer; the owner is responsible for keeping the computer secure.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Quan Ly Kenh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
