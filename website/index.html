Quan Ly Kenh

A private dashboard for YouTube channel owners, by Sonsay Media.

Quan Ly Kenh helps a YouTube creator keep track of the channels they own without opening YouTube Studio for every channel one by one. It runs locally on the owner's own computer and shows the owner's channel data in a single, simple screen.

What the app does

  • Shows channel statistics: subscribers, views, videos and daily growth.
  • Shows YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channels and videos: watch time, average view duration, traffic sources, countries, devices, audience retention and, where the channel is monetised, estimated revenue.
  • Lists public comments on the owner's videos that the channel has not replied to yet, and helps the owner translate them and draft replies. The app never posts anything on the owner's behalf.
  • Warns the owner when a channel needs attention (for example a sudden drop in views or revenue).

How Google account access works

The app asks the channel owner for read-only access to their own YouTube channel data through Google OAuth. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos, change channel settings, or post or delete comments. The owner can withdraw access at any time from their Google Account permissions page, or by disconnecting the channel inside the app.

Privacy. Data received from Google is stored only on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Quan Ly Kenh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
